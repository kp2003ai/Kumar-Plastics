Why moulded plastic

One piece. No weak joints.

Every product is formed in a single mould cycle, which is what makes it hold up to daily wear.

1-PC
Single-piece frame
No screws, welds or glue joints to work loose over time.
UV+
UV-stabilised
Colour and strength hold up under direct sun for outdoor use.
